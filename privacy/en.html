BYESHRIMP / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

MUISIK, the operator of ByeShrimp, respects your privacy. This policy explains the data the app processes on your device, how purchases and external services handle information, and how to contact the developer about privacy.

1. Operator and scope

This policy applies to the ByeShrimp iOS app, this website, and inquiries sent directly to the developer. The operator is MUISIK (the developer). The app has no ByeShrimp account registration or sign-in feature and does not use a data collection server operated by the developer.

2. Data processed

AirPods motion data

After you allow Motion & Fitness access, the app uses motion data from supported AirPods to display your current head orientation, register and compare postures, and provide posture reminders. While the app is open, it may process this data for posture display and registration before you start reminders. Once you start posture sensing, it may continue in the background.

Raw motion data and real-time posture comparison results are processed in your device's memory. They are not sent to the developer's servers or stored as an ongoing sensing history. When you save a posture, registration details, including reference orientations calculated from sensor data, are stored on your device.

Saved postures and app settings

The app stores posture names, reference orientations, registration times, whether sensing is enabled for each posture, and a shared display reference on your device. You can save up to three postures and edit or delete them in the app. Your language, sound and reminder interval preferences, guide completion status, and a time value used to check the trial period are also stored on your device.

Connection, sensing and call status

The app checks AirPods connection and sensing status. It also uses call status provided by iOS to pause posture reminders while a call is in progress. These states are processed in your device's memory and are not sent to the developer's servers. The app does not read or store phone numbers or the contents of calls.

Notifications and purchases

The app uses iOS local notifications to tell you about changes such as sensing ending. It does not create a push notification token or use its own notification server. Activating the free trial and purchasing lifetime access use Apple's StoreKit. The app checks transaction information provided by Apple, such as the product, purchase time and any revocation, to verify access to posture reminder features. Apple processes payments; the developer does not receive your card number.

Email inquiries

If you email the developer, your email address, display name (if provided), message and any attachments you send are used to respond to your inquiry. Inquiry emails may be stored in the email service and the developer's mailbox. There is no separate contact form or customer management database.

3. Purposes and retention

  • Live motion data and status are processed in device memory for posture display, registration, comparison and reminder control. They are not stored as an ongoing sensing or call history.
  • Posture registration details are stored so you can use them when you reopen the app. Deleting an individual posture removes it from the saved list. Section 5 explains how the display reference shared by saved postures is retained and removed.
  • App settings and usage state remain on your device until you change them or remove the app's data. Guide completion status and the time value used to check the trial period are stored separately from the posture list.
  • Apple transaction records are used to verify and restore purchases. Apple retains these records under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Sharing and external services

The developer does not sell data processed by the app or share it with third parties for advertising or usage analytics. The app does not use advertising or usage-analytics SDKs and does not send posture or sensor data to the developer's servers.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. It may also process technical information, such as browser or device details and request logs, to deliver and protect the site. The developer does not use a separate website analytics tool. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

In-app purchases and purchase restoration communicate with Apple. Apple's purchase, payment and device backup services are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Inquiry emails pass through your email service and Google's Gmail, which the developer uses. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's data processing.

5. Your choices and data deletion

You can change Motion & Fitness and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Changing a permission does not automatically delete saved postures or settings. You can delete postures and change preferences such as language and sound in the app.

If other postures remain, the shared reference orientation used for the display may remain after you delete an individual posture. Deleting all postures also removes this shared reference. Successfully registering a new posture replaces it with the new registration value.

You can use Delete App in iOS to remove the app's data from your device. Offload App may keep its documents and data. Depending on your device settings, saved data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. Existing backups must be managed separately through the relevant backup service.

Use Restore purchases in the app to restore a purchase, and Apple's procedures for Apple Account requests. The developer does not store posture data or sensor records on its servers, so there is no such server-side data for the developer to export or delete. For questions about data processing, use the contact details in section 8.

6. Security

The app keeps saved postures and settings in its dedicated storage on your device and processes live sensor data in memory. It relies on device locking and operating-system security features. Whether saved data is backed up depends on your device and backup settings.

Diagnostic records, such as feature status and error codes, may remain on the device. The app does not use a separate analytics service to send these records to the developer's servers. Inquiry emails are processed through email service providers' systems.

7. Changes to this policy

If app features or applicable requirements change, this page and its last-updated date will be revised. Important changes will be announced on this page or in app update information.
