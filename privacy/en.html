BYESHRIMP / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

The ByeShrimp developer respects your privacy. This policy explains information handled by the ByeShrimp iOS app on your device, purchases processed through Apple, and how to contact the developer.

1. Operator and scope

This policy applies to the ByeShrimp app and this website. The operator is the ByeShrimp developer (ABBY-0419). The app is designed to work without a developer-operated account server or data collection server.

2. Information handled by the app

AirPods motion information

When you grant iOS Motion & Fitness access and start a reminder session, the app uses supported AirPods motion information to check for a posture you registered. Sensor samples and live matching state are processed in device memory during the session and are not sent to a developer server.

Saved postures and settings

Posture names and reference directions you choose, along with related detection settings, are stored on your device to provide app features. You can save up to three postures and edit or delete them in the app.

Notifications and purchases

Session-status notices use iOS local notifications. The app does not create a push notification token or use its own notification server. Trial and lifetime purchases use StoreKit in the Apple App Store. The app checks transaction information provided by Apple to determine access. Apple processes payment information; the app developer does not receive your card number.

3. Purposes and retention

  • Motion information is used to match postures and provide reminders during the active session. Live samples are released from memory when the session ends.
  • Saved postures and settings remain on your device so your choices are available when you reopen the app. They remain until you delete them or remove the app's data.
  • Apple transaction records are used for purchase verification and restoration. Apple's retention is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.
  • If you contact the developer by email, your message may remain in the email service and mailbox for as long as needed to respond. The app has no separate contact form or customer database.

4. Sharing and external services

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. To deliver and protect the site, GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device details, and request logs. The developer does not operate a separate visitor analytics service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The developer does not sell information handled by the app or share it with third parties for advertising or analytics. Apple handles iOS permissions, App Store purchases, and operating-system services under its own policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

The app does not include a developer-operated analytics SDK, advertising SDK, user account, or custom server transmission feature.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can change Motion & Fitness access in iOS Settings, delete saved postures in the app, and manage app settings on your device. Use Apple's processes for purchase restoration and Apple Account requests. Because the app does not store posture data or sensor records on a developer server, there is no such server-side data for the developer to export or delete.

6. Security

The app keeps saved posture information and settings in its on-device app storage and processes live sensor samples in memory. Device passcode, operating-system security, and backup behavior depend on your device settings. Direct email inquiries pass through your email service provider.

7. Changes

If app features or applicable requirements change, this page will be updated with a revised effective date. Important changes may also be communicated through an app update or the public site.
