BYESHRIMP / PRIVACY & SUPPORT

Posture reminders,
kept personal.

ByeShrimp is an iPhone app that uses motion data from supported AirPods to remind you when a saved head tilt persists. Posture data is processed on your device and is not sent to developer servers. Saved data may be included in device backups.
